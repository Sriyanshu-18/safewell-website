Community, awareness and a shared purpose

The story behind Safewell

A vision shaped by care and action.

Through her social-work journey, Anjana Sahoo became interested in how women's well-being and livelihood opportunities can support one another.

Following training connected with the Sanitary Napkin Manufacturing Project, she helped build a business around sanitary napkin production and paid work for women.

Today, SHG women are paid employees who operate machinery and handle packaging as part of the work behind Safewell.
