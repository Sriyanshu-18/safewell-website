OUR STORY

A journey shaped by service, skills and possibility.

Safewell grew from a desire to connect women's well-being with opportunities for meaningful work.

The Vision

From social work to local enterprise.

Anjana Sahoo, proprietor of S.S. Industries, has a background in social work and a continuing interest in women's empowerment through community initiatives.

Her work through Tejaswinee Charitable Trust has informed a wider interest in dignity, well-being and opportunity.

After training connected with the Sanitary Napkin Manufacturing Project, the idea took shape to produce sanitary pads while creating paid employment for SHG women.

At the Safewell manufacturing unit in Jagatsinghpur, women employees operate machinery and handle packaging.

Safewell's journey is ongoing. The aim is to build trust with customers and partners while recognising the people behind the product.
