People, process and responsibility.

Safewell products are associated with a local manufacturing unit under S.S. Industries.

SHG women employed at the unit operate machinery and handle packaging. Their participation is an important part of the everyday work behind the Safewell brand.

The manufacturing unit is based in Jagatsinghpur, Odisha, connecting local enterprise with opportunities for women.
