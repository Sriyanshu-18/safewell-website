Our perspective

A local business with a wider purpose.

Safewell is a brand of S.S. Industries, based in Jagatsinghpur, Odisha. Its story is connected not only to sanitary napkins, but also to the people involved in making them.

SHG women are paid employees at the manufacturing unit. They take part in activities including machinery operation and packaging.

We see local employment and the recognition of people's work as meaningful parts of the Safewell story.

The initiative behind the brand

A commitment to women's empowerment.

Anjana Sahoo, proprietor of S.S. Industries, is also a social worker and the owner of Tejaswinee Charitable Trust.

Her work has involved women's empowerment initiatives and training connected with the Sanitary Napkin Manufacturing Project.

This background forms an important part of the story behind Safewell and its connection with women's participation in manufacturing.
