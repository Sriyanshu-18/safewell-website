Work and opportunity

Skilled work deserves recognition.

At the manufacturing unit in Jagatsinghpur, SHG women are paid employees. Their responsibilities include operating machinery and handling packaging.

Their contribution is an important part of the everyday work behind Safewell. We believe that the people involved in making a product deserve recognition for their skills and effort.

Safewell believes their contribution should be represented honestly and respectfully. This page can share first-person stories about work, skills and aspirations, with each employee's permission and approval of the final wording.
