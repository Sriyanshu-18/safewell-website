ABOUT SAFEWELL

A brand built around care and purpose.

Rooted in Jagatsinghpur, Odisha, Safewell brings together a sanitary napkin brand, local enterprise and meaningful work for women.

Who we are

Practical care.
Human purpose.

Safewell is a sanitary napkin brand operating under S.S. Industries, whose proprietor is Anjana Sahoo. The brand connects an everyday menstrual-hygiene product with a wider commitment to women's well-being and employment.

The manufacturing unit is located in Jagatsinghpur, Odisha. SHG women are employed and contribute to the work by operating machinery and handling packaging.

Safewell serves local retailers and institutional partners. This website is intended to share the brand's story and product information and to help interested organisations start a conversation with the business.
